# Quickstart: Check quality selection and the warning on a slow network

These steps check what the unit tests, the Go tests with ffmpeg run by `task check`, and the e2e tests
in `task test-e2e` cannot: playback on a throttled network (acceptance criteria 9 and 10 of parent
Issue #521) and the caps on hardware methods (Edge Case 6), both on real hardware. Record the result
in the implementation PR body.

## Prerequisites

- VVMDM running with `task dev` or `task preview`, with at least one video of 1080p or more that can
  play directly (H.264 MP4) imported (the files generated by `web/e2e/media-fixtures.mjs` may be
  reused).
- Chrome or Firefox developer tools (network throttling).
- For Edge Case 6, a host with a hardware encoder (the prerequisites of
  [specs/025-hardware-encoding/quickstart.md](../025-hardware-encoding/quickstart.md)).

## Steps

1. Open a 1080p video and open the quality menu in the control bar. Only "Original", "720p", "480p"
   and "360p" are present, and `1080p` is not (acceptance criterion 1).
2. Choose "480p" while playing. Playback continues from the same position, and the "Converting for
   playback" indicator shows that it is transcoding to 480p (acceptance criterion 2). In the developer
   tools console, `document.querySelector("video").videoHeight` is `480`.
3. Choose "360p" while paused. It stays paused at the same position (acceptance criterion 4).
4. At "480p", seek with the progress bar. Playback continues at 480p from the seek position, and
   `videoHeight` stays `480` (acceptance criterion 7).
5. Bitrate (acceptance criterion 3): receive about 20 seconds with
   `curl --max-time 20 -o out.mp4 --cookie "<session>" "http://localhost:8080/api/videos/<id>/transcode.mp4?startMs=0&quality=480p"`.
   A fragmented MP4 cut off mid-stream may not report `bit_rate` in `format` or `stream`, so compute
   it from the received packets.
   - `ffprobe -v error -select_streams v:0 -show_entries stream=width,height -of csv=p=0 out.mp4`
     shows a video short side of 480.
   - `ffprobe -v error -show_entries packet=pts_time,size -of csv=p=0 out.mp4 | awk -F, '$1!="N/A"{if(n==0||$1<a)a=$1;if($1>b)b=$1;s+=$2;n++} END{printf "%.0f kbps\n", s*8/(b-a)/1000}'`
     shows a combined video and audio average of about 1300 kbps or less
     ([contracts/transcode-quality-api.md §2](contracts/transcode-quality-api.md#2-per-quality-transcode-guarantees)).
     ffmpeg transcodes faster than real time, so divide by the span of packet timestamps, not by the
     time spent receiving.
6. Open another video (larger than 480p). It starts at "480p", and still does after a page reload.
   Open a video of 480p or less: it starts at "Original", and the menu has only "Original"
   (acceptance criterion 5, Edge Cases 1 and 2).
7. Switch back to "Original". A video that can play directly returns to direct playback (the
   "Converting for playback" indicator disappears; acceptance criterion 6).
8. Open as a guest (a browser without the owner's session). The quality can be chosen and the video
   plays at the chosen quality (acceptance criterion 8).
9. Warning (acceptance criteria 9–11): play a 1080p video at "Original" and throttle the network in
   the developer tools to a speed below the original bitrate (for example "Slow 3G" or a custom
   1 Mbps profile). When 3 data waits occur within 60 seconds (or one lasts more than 10 seconds), a
   warning appears over the player. Playback does not stop, and the control bar and the centre
   controls (on touch devices) stay usable. The warning has no action that switches quality, and the
   quality has not changed. After dismissing it, it does not show again while that video plays. Open
   another video under the same conditions and it shows again.
10. With the network still throttled, seek, pause and resume. The loading waits right after those
    alone do not produce the warning (Edge Case 8).
11. Edge Case 6 (only on a host with hardware): choose a hardware method in the settings screen and
    repeat step 5. The short side and the average computed from packets fall in the same range. The
    host's process list shows `-maxrate 1200k`.
